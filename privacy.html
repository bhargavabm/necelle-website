Project and team

This site is for the Automated Nacelle Alignment System by UVCE Bengaluru, Mechanical Engineering.

What this site collects

The contact form does not send any data to a server. When you submit it, the page opens your own email app with the message ready to send. The team receives only what you choose to send from your own email.

The simulation sliders, presets, and buttons keep their settings in the page memory only. Those settings are lost when the page is closed. No form data or simulation setting is stored by this site.

External requests

The simulation page loads Three.js and Chart.js from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Your browser sends its IP address and standard browser information to that service when the page loads.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel keeps standard server request logs. Those logs may include your IP address, the page you requested, and the time of the request. Read Vercel's privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. It uses no analytics and no advertising. It does not use localStorage or sessionStorage.

Ask for removal

If you have sent us a message and want it removed, email bhargavabm2007@gmail.com with the subject "Removal request". The team will delete the message from the team mailbox.
