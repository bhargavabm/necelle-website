Automated Nacelle Alignment System

Automated Nacelle Alignment System

Keeps a wind turbine facing the wind to capture the most energy.

Open live simulation

The problem

Wind direction changes constantly.

A turbine produces the most power when its rotor faces the wind.

The nacelle must turn on the yaw bearing to stay aligned.

How it works

  1. 1

    Detect wind

    A wind vane and anemometer on the nacelle roof measure direction and speed.

  2. 2

    Compare with nacelle heading

    Misalignment equals wind direction minus nacelle heading.

  3. 3

    Controller computes yaw command

    A PD law, then a deadband check.

  4. 4

    Yaw motor turns nacelle

    The yaw drive turns the nacelle through the yaw bearing, with a rate limit.

  5. 5

    Aligned, power resumes

    Within ±5°, the turbine produces full power.
